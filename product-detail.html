Brighten, Firm & Glow

Our premium Vitamin C Serum is formulated with 10% Ethyl-Ascorbic Acid (SAP — stable Vitamin C), combined with Niacinamide, Hyaluronic Acid, and Ferulic Acid. Dermatologist and PCSIR-tested. Brightens complexion, reduces dark spots, fights aging, and deeply hydrates. Fragrance-free and paraben-free. 30ml bottle.

  • Brightens and evens skin tone
  • Reduces dark spots and hyperpigmentation
  • Boosts collagen for firmer skin
  • Deep hydration with Hyaluronic Acid
  • Fragrance-free & paraben-free
  • Suitable for all skin types

Key Ingredients

  • Vitamin C (SAP 10%) — stable, non-irritating form of Vitamin C
  • Niacinamide — brightens, reduces pores, calms inflammation
  • Hyaluronic Acid — intense hydration, plumping effect
  • Ferulic Acid — antioxidant booster, enhances Vitamin C stability

How to Use

Step 1: Cleanse face thoroughly
Step 2: Apply 3–4 drops of serum to face and neck
Step 3: Gently pat in upward strokes until absorbed
Step 4: Follow with moisturizer and SPF (daytime)

Note: Use morning and/or evening. Avoid direct eye contact.
