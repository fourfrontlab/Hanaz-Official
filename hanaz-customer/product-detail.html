Product Description

Brighten, Firm & Glow

Our premium serum is formulated with carefully selected active ingredients. Hyaluronic Acid, and Ferulic Acid. Dermatologist-tested and PCSIR-tested for quality and safety.

Key Ingredients

Vitamin C (SAP 10%)

Stable, non-irritating Vitamin C that brightens skin and supports collagen production.

Niacinamide

Helps reduce enlarged pores, calm inflammation, and improve overall skin tone.

Hyaluronic Acid

Holds up to 1,000× its weight in water for deep, lasting hydration and a plump complexion.

Your Routine

How to Use & Care

How to Use

Follow these simple steps every morning and evening for best results.

  1. 01 Cleanse your face thoroughly.
  2. 02 Apply 2–3 drops to dry face and neck.
  3. 03 Gently massage until fully absorbed.
  4. 04 Follow with your moisturizer.
  5. 05 Finish with SPF 30+ during daytime.

Skin & Safety

Follow these important safety recommendations before using your serum.

  1. 01 Perform a patch test 24 hours prior to first use.
  2. 02 Formulated exclusively for topical facial application.
  3. 03 Avoid direct contact with eyes and open wounds.
  4. 04 Discontinue use if persistent irritation occurs.
  5. 05 Consult a physician if pregnant or nursing.

Storage & Care

Store properly to ensure maximum formula potency and longevity.

  1. 01 Store in a cool, dry place below 25°C (77°F).
  2. 02 Keep away from direct sunlight and humidity.
  3. 03 Seal dropper cap tightly after each application.
  4. 04 Do not freeze or expose to temperature extremes.
  5. 05 Best used within 6 months of initial opening.
